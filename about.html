About the Project

{{ site.title }} is a project of {{ site.machon_name }} מכון ארצות הברית הקדמון, alongside Early American Rabbis and Early American Jewish History.

Long before the great Torah centers of today, Jews built kehillos in cities and small towns across America. Each one had its rav, its shul, its mikvah and its cheder, and each left a record in newspapers, minute books and memories.

For every community on the map, the project gathers the rabbanim who served there, the shuls, the mikvaos, the institutions of chinuch, the cemeteries, and interesting news and events from the community’s history, with photographs and original documents wherever they survive.

The rabbanim listed on each community page come from two places: rabbanim entered for that community here, and every rabbi on the Early American Rabbis site who served there.

Have a photograph, a document or a story about one of these communities? We would be glad to hear from you.
